🧵 1/7 The Architecture Behind @GrowApp on Solana: How sub-second yields and programmatic asset rebalancing are engineered on the SVM.

A deep technical dive into Grow's vault state machine, PDA isolation, and atomic CPI orchestration ⚡️👇

---

2/7 🏛️ State Isolation & Deterministic Vault PDAs
Grow doesn't use centralized custody or custodial smart contracts. Every vault is a deterministic Program-Derived Address (PDA):
`seeds = [b"grow_vault", authority.key().as_ref(), mint.key().as_ref()]`

Zero off-chain custody risk. All state transitions occur within Solana's Sealevel runtime.

---

3/7 🔄 Atomic Rebalancing via Cross-Program Invocations (CPI)
When Grow reallocates capital to optimize APR:
1. Epoch/slot check prevents stale price oracle poisoning (Pyth/Switchboard verification).
2. Native Token Program CPI executes single-slot transfers to target pools (Raydium/Meteora/Kamino).
3. LP or yield shares are minted directly to the vault PDA. No intermediate approvals required.

---

4/7 🛡️ Reentrancy Resistance & Dynamic Fee Guards
Solana's architecture eliminates EVM-style call stack reentrancy by design, but cross-instruction state manipulation remains a threat.
Grow guards against this by enforcing:
• Mutex account state locks during rebalancing
• Slippage bounds derived from dynamic volatility metrics
• Compute Unit (CU) budgeting capped at ~42,000 CU per deposit

---

5/7 ⚡ MEV & Jito Bundle Integration
Volatile rebalances on Solana can fall victim to sandwich attacks in the mempool.
Grow keeper bots package vault harvests and rebalances into atomic Jito MEV bundles with tip programs:
• Guarantees first-look execution in the block
• Zero partial fills or frontrun slippage
• 100% of realized yield flows back to vault share owners

---

6/7 📊 Tokenized Yield Shares (SPL-Token 2022 Ready)
Depositing into Grow deposits collateral and mints `gTokens` (Yield-Bearing Receipt Tokens).
Share math enforces constant-ratio parity:
`Shares_To_Mint = (Deposit_Amount * Total_Shares) / Total_Vault_Assets`
Compounded returns auto-accrue into the token exchange rate without taxable transaction events.

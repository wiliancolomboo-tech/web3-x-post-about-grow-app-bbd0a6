`['grow_vault', user_pubkey]`

2/ Sub-400ms High-Frequency Compounding

On Solana, a complete Grow deposit + compound loop consumes only 21,800 Compute Units (~$0.00035).

3/ Eliminating the Inflation Attack
Classic vault designs (like early ERC-4626 implementations) suffer from share donation / inflation attacks. 

Grow resolves this deterministically:

4/8 Institutional-Grade Security Architecture 🛡️

Yield protocols are prime targets for flash-loan sandwich attacks. Grow mitigates this through 3 core safeguards:
1. Oracle & Yield Slippage Guards (< 50 bps bounds).
2. Minimum time-weighted harvest epochs (anti-reentrancy buffer).
3. Hardcoded PDA signer seeds for share burn/mint authority.
